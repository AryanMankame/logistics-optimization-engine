Methodology & Assumptions

Turnover = Σ orders.total_amount, where each line is item selling price × qty. Goods cost (COGS) = Σ item buy-cost × qty from fulfilled splits — each item carries its own margin (selling price vs buy cost, default 10% when not configured). Gross profit = turnover − COGS. Delivery income = per-km fee × radial warehouse→house km per fulfilled split (0 when free delivery). Each dispatch is a real van tour (warehouse → nearest house → next … → back to warehouse, greedy nearest-stop); fuel and trip time use the full tour distance, while the customer fee stays quoted on radial distance. Operational cost = warehouse maintenance (warehouses × runDays × ₹/day) + fuel (Σ tour distance × L/km × ₹/L) + packaging (orders × ₹/order). Net profit = gross + delivery income − operational. Required fee, two jobs: job 1 — the rate must at least cover fuel (floor = L/km × ₹/L); job 2 — product margin + delivery margin must lift net ÷ total cost to the target return. Recommended rate is the higher of the two, so delivery always pays its own fuel. Warehouse profit = its turnover − its goods cost − maintenance − dispatch fuel − packaging. Vehicle profit = attributed turnover − attributed goods cost − vehicle fuel (internal allocation; excludes maintenance/packaging). Delivery time = order_delivered − order_created (order level). Note: this simulation fulfills every order within the same hour it is created, so order-level lead time is 0h by design — see vehicle hours-out for the meaningful duration signal. Idle-by-hour uses dispatch start hour + duration windows. Furthest locations use straight-line (haversine) distance to the nearest warehouse.
